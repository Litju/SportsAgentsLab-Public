# Responsive Scope Amendment

Desktop-first is a prioritization rule, not permission for broken smaller screens.

## Required

- Core navigation remains reachable on tablet/smaller browser widths.
- Evidence state, uncertainty labels, and practitioner decision controls remain semantically complete.
- Tables/charts may stack, scroll, or enter focused views without removing provenance or status.
- Agent dock may collapse to a drawer/panel but session continuity remains.

## Not required for v1

- Touch-first metric exploration.
- Phone-first trial trace adjudication.
- Native push notifications.
- On-device force-plate connectivity.
