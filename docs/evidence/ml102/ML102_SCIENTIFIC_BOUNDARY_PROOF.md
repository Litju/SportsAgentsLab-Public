# ML-102 Scientific Boundary Proof

ML-102 stores bytes, identity, provenance, and bounded import-envelope status
only. Every accepted attempt remains `scientific_eligibility=INELIGIBLE` and
ends at `READY_FOR_ADAPTER`; unsupported and malformed envelopes cannot enter
that state as scientifically eligible data.

The implementation contains no vendor adapter, force-column interpretation,
CMJ calculation, body-weight estimation, jump-height calculation, readiness,
fatigue, injury-risk, or meaningful-change inference. No raw source bytes or
force traces are sent to an LLM. Historical authority documents and negative
boundary tests may mention forbidden terms; those references are not product
calculations.

`B02_STATUS=DEFERRED` remains unchanged. ML-103 and all scientific blocks stay
gated on owner acceptance of ML-102.
