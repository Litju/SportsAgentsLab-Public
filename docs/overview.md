# What SportsAgentsLab is

SportsAgentsLab is an agentic sports-science system for measurement, research,
monitoring, and training decision support. Specialized agents operate within
defined scientific and operational domains while practitioners retain
authority over consequential decisions.

This public snapshot is a curated technical view of a privately developed
system. It contains selected real source code, small public adapters,
deterministic scientific examples, and synthetic demonstrations. It does not
represent the complete product or every supported measurement system.

## Design principles

- Deterministic numerical software produces measurements; language models do
  not act as measurement calculators.
- Evidence, provenance, uncertainty, and limitations remain visible.
- Agent boundaries are typed and reviewable.
- Synthetic data keeps the showcase reproducible and privacy-safe.
- Practitioners remain the decision authority.
